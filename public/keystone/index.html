How it works

One job, one record, from enquiry to paid.

Every job follows the same path. The client, the scope and the price carry across each step, so nobody re-enters them and nothing gets lost or changed on the way.

1

Sales project

The client, the notes and the follow-ups.

2

Estimate

Priced in your layout, as a PDF with stone photos.

3

Client signs

Sent for signing. You see when it was opened.

4

Project

Created from the won quote, with its costs and margin.

5

Invoiced, paid

Invoices come from the quote’s payment stages.

01

Win the work

Every opportunity sits on one board with its stage, its value and who owns it. Open one and the client, estimates, samples, notes and follow-ups are all together.

  • Leads, quotes and won work in one list
  • Follow-up reminders on every quote
  • Duplicates merged, pipeline exported in one click
02

Price it, in your own layout

Build the estimate by floor, room or area, with materials, finishes and installation. It goes out as a branded PDF with photos of the stone. Every earlier version is kept.

  • Quantities in m², linear metres, days or units
  • Headings, notes and descriptions
  • PDF and Excel export
03

Deliver the job

A won quote becomes a project. Its timeline runs from quotation through templating, shop drawings, fabrication, shipping and installation to snagging and handover, with a checklist and the next action always visible.

  • Tasks, documents and site photos by project
  • Snags with a photo and an owner
  • One-click client report
04

See the margin while the job runs

Contract value, income received, costs by category and gross margin, worked out for you as expenses come in. You find out a job is losing money while you can still do something about it.

05

Get paid

Three steps: create the invoice from a payment stage, send it, tick it off when it is paid. Overdue invoices are flagged with the number of days, and paying one marks its stage paid in finance.

  • Numbered PDF invoices from the quote
  • Who owes what, and how late
  • Money in and out across every project in one list

About

Why I built it.

I work in natural stone, pricing packages for high-end homes, hotels, shops and commercial projects.

Across the trade, the same thing happens again and again: jobs live in spreadsheets, chats and email. Quotes end up in several versions, samples are hard to track and the margin on a job is only known late. Most of the software out there was not made for this kind of work.

I wanted one place where a job could be followed from the first enquiry to the final invoice, built around how stone firms really work. So I built it.

Pietro Sacchelli, founder. I trained as an architect (BArch, University of Westminster, 2024) and worked at Laing O’Rourke, BASTET Design House and Studio NP before moving into stone. Reading drawings, doing take-offs, working out slab yields and pricing specialist materials is what I know, so Keystone is built the way an estimator actually works. I designed and built it myself.

Questions

Things firms usually ask.

Can the estimates look like ours?

Yes. Estimates come out in your company’s own layout, with your logo, your headings and photos of the stone, as a PDF or Excel file.

We already have years of contacts and jobs in Excel. Can we bring them in?

Yes. Contacts and companies are brought in during setup, and some reports, like the weekly sample report, import straight from Excel.

Does it work for the people on site?

Yes. It runs on a phone, installs to the home screen like an app and sends notifications. Snags, photos and stage updates can all be done on site.

Who can see what?

Each person has a role: admin, manager or user. The database itself enforces what each role can see, not just the screens.

What if we want to leave?

Your data exports to Excel at any time. There is no lock-in.

We are not a stone firm. Is it for us?

Stone, terrazzo and ceramics fit best today. Joinery, metalwork and glazing fit well and are next. If you make and fit custom materials job by job, it is worth a conversation.
